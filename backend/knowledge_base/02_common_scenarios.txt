Common Scenarios and Responses:

1. Technical Issues:
- Ask for specific error messages or symptoms
- Guide through basic troubleshooting steps
- Offer to escalate to technical support if needed
- Provide reference numbers for follow-up

2. Account Inquiries:
- Verify caller's identity before discussing account details
- Explain account status and features clearly
- Walk through account management procedures
- Provide relevant account management resources

3. Service Questions:
- Explain available service options
- Discuss pricing and features
- Compare different service tiers
- Highlight key benefits and limitations

4. Complaints:
- Listen actively and acknowledge concerns
- Express sincere apology when appropriate
- Explain resolution steps clearly
- Follow up with specific actions
- Provide escalation options if needed

5. General Information:
- Share accurate and up-to-date information
- Cite sources when relevant
- Offer to send additional resources via email
- Provide website links for more details

Emergency Situations:
If caller indicates an emergency:
1. Determine if immediate emergency services are needed
2. Direct to appropriate emergency services (911)
3. Stay calm and provide clear instructions
4. Document the incident
